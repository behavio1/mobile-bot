# Summarization

Read the full material in scope and identify its purpose. Preserve decisions, required actions, owners, dates, constraints, evidence, and open questions. Keep facts separate from interpretations and retain uncertainty present in the source.

Choose a structure suited to the request: a short narrative for a simple update, or grouped points when several parallel decisions or actions need scanning. Remove repetition and background that does not affect meaning. Do not merge distinct claims or add conclusions absent from the source.

When the source is incomplete, truncated, or internally inconsistent, say so in the summary. Include references to the original files, messages, or links when the reader may need to verify details.
